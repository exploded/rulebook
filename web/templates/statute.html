Who made them

Acts made each year, by the side of politics in government on the day each Act was made. Of all Acts since 1901, {{count .MadeNonLabor}} were made under non-Labor governments and {{count .MadeLabor}} under Labor governments.

An Act is made on the day it receives Royal Assent. Parliament passes it, so the opposition and crossbench often vote for it too. "Non-Labor" covers the Protectionist, Free Trade, Commonwealth Liberal, National Labor, Nationalist, United Australia, Country and Liberal parties. How we assign governments.
