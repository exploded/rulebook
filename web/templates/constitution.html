The Constitution

The Constitution

The Commonwealth of Australia Constitution Act was passed by the British Parliament in 1900 and took effect on 1 January 1901. Its ninth clause holds the Constitution: {{.Sections}} sections in 8 chapters, and a schedule. Only the people can change it, by a referendum under section 128.

What the Parliament may make laws about

Section 51 lists the subjects on which the Commonwealth Parliament may make laws. Anything not listed here, or given to the Commonwealth elsewhere in the Constitution, stays with the States. The High Court decides how far each head of power reaches.
